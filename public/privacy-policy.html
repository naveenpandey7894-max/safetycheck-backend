1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SafetyCheck ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use the SafetyCheck mobile application and its related services (the "Service"). SafetyCheck helps workers report workplace safety issues with a photo and location, and helps supervisors track and resolve them.

By using the Service you acknowledge that you have read this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Service.

2. Organisations and your data

SafetyCheck is provided to organisations such as employers and facility operators ("Your Organisation"). Your account is normally created for you by Your Organisation. Reports you submit are visible to authorised people in Your Organisation, for example supervisors and administrators, depending on their role. Your Organisation decides who has access and may have its own policies about the reports and data you submit, so some requests about your data may need to be handled together with Your Organisation.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information

  • Name and email address
  • Password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form)
  • Role (for example Worker, Supervisor, or Admin) and the organisation you belong to

3.2 Report content you provide

  • Photos captured with the in-app camera
  • The site you select, a free-text location within the site, and optional notes
  • Report status and assignment information as your report is handled

3.3 Location information

  • Precise GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) at the time you create a report
  • An approximate street address derived from those coordinates (see Section 7)

We access your location only while the app is in use, and only to attach it to a report.

3.4 AI-generated information

For each photo we generate a suggested category, severity level, confidence score, and a short text description of what the photo appears to show.

3.5 Technical information

  • IP address, request and error logs, and timestamps created when the app communicates with our servers
  • An authentication token stored on your device so you stay signed in until you log out

3.6 What we do not collect

The app does not access your microphone, contacts, or photo library. Photos are taken only through the in-app camera.

5. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in
  • To receive, store, display, and track safety reports, and to route them to the right people
  • To generate an AI-suggested category and severity for each report
  • To show reports and statistics (for example dashboards) to authorised users in Your Organisation
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse, and investigate false or fraudulent reports
  • To maintain, debug, and improve the Service
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

6. AI processing of your photos

When you submit a report, the photo is sent to a third-party AI vision service, which returns the suggested category, severity, and description. The provider handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy. AI suggestions can be wrong and are meant to assist, not replace, human judgement; a person may review and correct them.

Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or a valid government request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, Your Organisation, or others.

Business changes. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. International transfers

Some of our service providers may store or process data on servers in countries other than the one where you live. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps so that your information stays protected in line with this policy and applicable law.

9. How long we keep information

We keep account and report data while your account is active and for as long as Your Organisation needs it for its safety and compliance records. After that, or when a valid deletion request is approved, we delete or anonymise it, unless we must keep it to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or preserve workplace safety records.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashing of passwords, role-based access controls, and token-based authentication. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details private and log out on shared devices.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • request deletion of your account and information
  • withdraw consent you previously gave (this will not affect past processing)
  • raise a complaint about how your data is handled

To make a request, including a request to delete your account and data, contact us using the details in Section 14 or ask your Organisation's administrator. We may need to verify your identity, and we may need to involve Your Organisation in handling your request. We aim to respond within 30 days.

12. Children

The Service is intended for working adults and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by another suitable method. Continuing to use the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
